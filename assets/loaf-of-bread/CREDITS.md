# Credits — *How a loaf of bread cost 200 billion marks*

The finished video is **CC BY-SA 3.0**, because 13 of its 25 photographs are. That is not a
choice about this video so much as a consequence of the archive: the German federal archive
(Bundesarchiv) released its Weimar Republic photographs under CC BY-SA, and they are the only
pictures of the thing this film is about. `stock_search` refuses ShareAlike by default and was
called here with `licence: "allow-sharealike"`, which is the explicit opt-in for exactly this.
