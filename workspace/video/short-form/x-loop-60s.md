# The loop — 60 seconds, silent

**Platform:** X, the home page, the repo README
**Length:** 55 to 60s, cut to loop cleanly
**Format:** screen recording, two panes, no voice, no music
**Goal:** stop the scroll, prove the claim, send them to `/status`

> Every timing below is provisional until step 0 of `_video-plan.md` produces real wall-clock
> numbers. Shoot first, then cut to these beats if the material allows it.

---

## The hook problem, and how this solves it

The playbook's hook formulas are contrarian claims, curiosity gaps and bold statements. All of
them are banned here: `VOICE.md` forbids promotional register, and the studio's whole position
is that it is not doing the hype thing.

**So the hook is not a sentence. It is the thing happening.** A timeline assembling itself while
a conversation scrolls beside it is a pattern interrupt on its own, and it is the one hook
formula in the table that survives contact with this brand: *visual surprise*. It also happens
to be the honest one, because it is just the product working.

The first frame is not a title card. It is the app, mid-build.

---

## HOOK — 0 to 3s

**Visual:** Cold open on the two panes, already in motion. A tool call lands on the left, a
shape lands on the timeline on the right, in the same beat.

**On screen (0.5s in, one line, bottom left, small):**

> Nobody touched the timeline.

**Direction:** Start the capture *after* the first two calls have already landed. Opening on an
empty project is three wasted seconds, and there are only sixty.

**Why it holds:** the viewer cannot immediately tell what is driving it, and the caption tells
them the one thing that makes it strange.

## BODY — 3 to 45s

Four beats. Each is one cut, each carries one line of text, and each line is a fact rather than
a claim.

**Beat 1 (3 to 14s) — the calls are real**
Left pane in focus. The agent's tool calls scroll past: `add_shape`, `animate`, `add_text`.

> `add_shape` · `animate` · `add_text`

**Beat 2 (14 to 26s) — it lands where you can reach it**
Right pane in focus. Clips appear on the timeline. The `● Claude` chip fires and fades.

> Each call is the same command your click makes.

**Beat 3 (26 to 34s) — the part nobody else can show**
**This is the beat the whole video exists for.** Take the mouse. Drag one clip the agent placed.
Then press ⌘Z and let the agent's own edit come off the stack.

> ⌘Z undoes its edits like any other.

**Beat 4 (34 to 45s) — it finishes**
`render` fires. Cut to the MP4 playing full frame, the reel's own audio unused because the video
is silent.

> 21.429s · 1080×1920 · 30fps · h264 and AAC

**Direction:** beat 3 is worth more than the other three combined. If the take is good
everywhere else and weak there, re-shoot. Every competitor can show a machine producing a video.
None of them can show you taking it back.

## CLOSE — 45 to 58s

**Visual:** the finished reel finishes playing. Hold two seconds on the last frame. Cut to a
plain card on the paper background from the site.

**On screen:**

> A video editor for the Mac.
> In development, and honest about which parts.
>
> bay.everholt.com/status

**Direction:** the card is typeset in the site's own paper world, so the video and the page it
links to are visibly the same object. No logo animation. No "coming soon".

**Loop point:** cut back to frame one on a hard cut. Since the close is a static card and the
open is mid-motion, the loop reads as deliberate rather than broken.

**Typesetting:** the site's own faces and palette. The captions are part of the argument that
the site, the app and the video are one thing.

**Accessibility:** the video is silent by design, so the on-screen text is the whole soundtrack.
Keep every line above 4.5:1 against what is behind it, and measure it rather than eyeballing it.
The reel recipe's own craft note applies here: `#26262E` on `#0E0E12` measures 1.28:1 and is
invisible, and opacity defeats the measurement.

## The X post that carries it

Text, under 280 characters, no thread, no hook formula:

> This is bay building a 21 second reel. The calls on the left are an agent's. The timeline on
> the right is the one I can drag clips around on. Same commands, same undo stack, all on this
> Mac.
>
> No download yet. What runs today: bay.everholt.com/status

**Notes**
- No hashtags. On X they read as a reach play and this audience discounts them.
- No "🧵" and no thread. The video is the argument.
- Post the video natively. An external link in the same post suppresses reach and, more to the
  point, a player that phones home would contradict the product.
- **Say "no download yet" in the post.** It costs nothing and it stops the top reply being
  "where do I get it", which is the reply that makes the thread about absence.
