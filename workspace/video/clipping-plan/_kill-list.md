## Rejected on brand grounds, whatever they would score

| Candidate | Why |
|---|---|
| A staged failure and recovery | A re-creation. See `clips-from-the-build.md`, clip 2. This is the one project that cannot ship one |
| Side-by-side against a named competitor | Punching at CapCut or Descript with no users and no download is a bad look and invites a comparison you would currently lose on features |
| A speed ramp over the render with no caption | Breaks the standard the site sets on its own sequence |
| "What if I told you..." or any hook formula opener | Banned register. The product is the hook |
| A countdown, a launch date, or "coming soon" | No date exists. `website/CLAUDE.md` forbids the standing-in-for-a-download pattern and the spirit applies to video |
| Anything shot on a machine where the app was pre-warmed off camera | If the state was prepared, the take starts before the recording does, and that is the thing this whole plan is trying not to do |
