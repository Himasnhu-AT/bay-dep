# Budget allocation

**Stated budget: $0/month, time only.** So this page allocates the 15+ hrs/week, which is
the resource that actually exists, and names the one cash line that becomes unavoidable.

Benchmark context: pre-revenue companies are expected to run at $0–500/month with founder
time as the real input. bay is at the bottom of that band by choice, which is fine at this
stage and would not be by Month 6.

---

## Time allocation (the real budget)

| Activity | % | Hrs/week | Front-loaded to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer interviews + synthesis | 30% | ~4.5 | Month 1, tapering after |
| Demo capture and re-cuts | 25% | ~4 | Weeks 1–2 heavy, then maintenance |
| Build in public + community | 20% | ~3 | Constant, or it does not compound |
| Writing (site copy, quickstart, 2 pieces) | 15% | ~2.5 | Weeks 4–8 |
| Distribution mechanics (repo public, MCP listings, list) | 10% | ~1.5 | Weeks 3–4, mostly one-time |

Two notes on this split. Interviews get the largest share because the stage's job is
learning, not reach. Build-in-public is the only line that must not be batched away — three
posts a week for twelve weeks compounds, and twelve posts in week eleven does not.

## The 70/20/10 rule, translated to $0

The standard split is 70% proven channels, 20% testing, 10% experimental. bay has no proven
channel, so the split collapses:

- **0% proven** — nothing has been validated yet
- **~70% testing** — interviews, the demo, build-in-public, MCP directories. All of these
  are tests with named success criteria, not programmes
- **~30% asset-building** — the demo recording and the two engineering pieces retain value
  regardless of which channel wins

Revisit this split the moment one channel produces a second qualified signup you did not
manually source. That is the signal to start concentrating.

---

## When to start spending

Do not raise the budget on a schedule. Raise it on a trigger.

| Trigger | Then spend on |
|---|---|
| 100 qualified signups from one identifiable channel | Nothing yet. Work that channel harder by hand first |
| 10+ non-founder installs, 3+ rendered-and-kept | Notarization, code signing, and whatever the first installs proved was broken |
| A repeatable channel producing signups without manual work | A small test budget on that channel only, $200–500/month, with a named kill date |
| Revenue exists | Re-run `/kai-growth-plan`. Different stage, different rules |

Spending before the second trigger is buying reach for a product a stranger cannot obtain.
